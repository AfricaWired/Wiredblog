A dashboard nobody opens after the first week usually has the same root cause: it shows everything the underlying data model can produce, not what the person using it needs to decide something.

Before we design a screen, we ask what action someone should be able to take after looking at it. If the answer is "nothing, it is just informational," that is usually a sign the screen should not exist as a dashboard at all — a scheduled report or an alert does the job better.

The dashboards that stay in daily use are narrow on purpose: a handful of numbers that answer a specific recurring question, not a wall of charts.
