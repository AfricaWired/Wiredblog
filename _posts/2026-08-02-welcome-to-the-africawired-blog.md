AfricaWired is a software engineering company based in Sakina, Arusha, Tanzania. We design, build and run software for businesses across Africa — from custom web and mobile applications to cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, data and AI systems.

This blog is where our engineering team will share what we are building, the technical decisions behind it, and lessons learned from running production systems for our clients. Expect posts on software architecture, cloud and DevOps practices, application security, and applied AI — grounded in real project work rather than theory.

Thanks for reading, and welcome aboard.
