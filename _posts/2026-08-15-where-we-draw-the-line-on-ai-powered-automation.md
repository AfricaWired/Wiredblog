Clients often ask us to "add AI" to a workflow without a specific problem attached to the request. Our first question back is always what decision is currently slow, expensive, or inconsistent enough that automating it is worth the added complexity.

AI-powered automation earns its place well in high-volume, pattern-heavy tasks — document classification, anomaly flagging in operational data, drafting first-pass responses a human still reviews. It earns its place poorly in low-volume decisions with real consequences, where a wrong call is expensive and hard to audit after the fact.

We design these systems so a human can see why a recommendation was made and override it — not as a black box bolted onto an existing process.
